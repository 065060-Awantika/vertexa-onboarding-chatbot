Communication and Escalation Flow - Vertexa Systems

Vertexa Systems follows a structured communication framework to ensure smooth collaboration between internal teams and client-facing stakeholders.

Two-Way Communication Model
- Incoming Path: Client requests and inputs flow through designated channels to the relevant technical teams for execution and delivery.
- Outgoing Path: Updates and deliverables flow back through a structured approval process to client stakeholders for review.

Official Channels
- Email is used for formal documentation and tracking of requests, approvals, and decisions.
- Microsoft Teams and calls are used for real-time discussion and immediate clarifications.
- Dedicated Single Points of Contact (SPOCs) ensure proper escalation paths and accountability.

Typical Escalation Path (Incoming requests from a client)
Client SPOC -> Helpdesk / Program Management -> relevant Technical Team Lead (e.g. DevOps TL, Database TL, Cloud TL, or other specialized leads) -> Technical team member assigned to resolve the request.

Typical Escalation Path (Outgoing updates to a client)
Technical team -> respective Team Lead -> Program/Delivery Management -> Client SPOC.

Why This Matters for New Employees
- Always route formal requests and confirmations through email so there is a documented trail.
- Use Teams/calls only for quick clarifications, not as a replacement for documented approvals.
- If unsure who to escalate an issue to, contact the Helpdesk or your reporting manager first - they will route it to the correct Team Lead.
- This dual-channel approach balances efficiency (fast clarification) with accountability (documented tracking).

General IT Helpdesk Contact
For any IT-related issue (access, software, hardware, account setup), raise a ticket through the IT Portal or email the IT Helpdesk. If it is urgent, follow up with your reporting manager.
