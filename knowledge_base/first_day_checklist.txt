First Day Checklist - Vertexa Systems

Before Your First Day
- Confirm your joining date, reporting location (or remote setup instructions), and reporting time with HR.
- Keep your ID proof and any documents requested by HR ready (as communicated in your offer/joining letter).

On Your First Day
1. Report to HR at the designated time and location to complete joining formalities.
2. Collect your laptop and any hardware from the IT desk.
3. Confirm access to your official email account and company communication tools (Microsoft Teams, etc.).
4. Meet your reporting manager and get an overview of your team and initial responsibilities.
5. Complete any mandatory compliance/HR documentation (ID proof submission, bank details for payroll, etc.)

First Week
- Get access to project-specific tools as assigned by your Team Lead/manager (see Technology Landscape and IT Setup Guide documents).
- Understand the communication and escalation flow for your project (see Communication and Escalation document).
- Review the Leave and Attendance Policy and note the shared leave calendar.
- Note the reimbursement submission deadline (18th of every month) if applicable to your role.
- Familiarize yourself with the Code of Conduct expectations.

Who to Contact for Onboarding Questions
- General HR/joining formalities: HR team
- Laptop, email, VPN, or software access: IT Helpdesk
- Team-specific tools, project assignment, responsibilities: Reporting manager or Team Lead
