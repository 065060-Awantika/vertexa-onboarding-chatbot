Technology Landscape - Vertexa Systems

New team members work across a wide technology stack. Here is an overview of what you may encounter on projects:

1. Physical Layer
Servers, storage, backup devices, switches, routers, load balancers, and security devices.

2. Cloud and Control Layer
Private Cloud, Public Cloud, and Hybrid Cloud environments. Tools and platforms include OpenStack, OpenShift, OpenShift AI, Ansible, Terraform, AWS, and Azure. Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) and monitoring tools are used extensively.

3. Cybersecurity
Ransomware Protection, Anti-DDoS, WAF (Web Application Firewall), IDAM (Identity and Access Management), SIEM, SOAR, NAC, DLP, and Vulnerability Assessment (VA).

4. Operating Systems
RedHat Linux, Ubuntu Linux, SUSE Linux, and Windows.

5. Databases
SQL: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Oracle DB.
NoSQL: MongoDB, Redis, Cassandra, Hadoop, Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, ScyllaDB, and other distributed NoSQL systems.

6. Message Brokers
Kafka and RabbitMQ.

Technology Partnerships
Vertexa Systems maintains OEM partnerships with several leading global infrastructure, cloud, and security vendors. Employees may work with any of these technologies depending on the project and team assignment.

New joiners are encouraged to check with their reporting manager to understand which specific stack applies to their project, and request relevant tool access through the IT Portal (see IT Setup Guide).
