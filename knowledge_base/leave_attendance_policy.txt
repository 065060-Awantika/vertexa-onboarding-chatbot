Leave and Attendance Policy - Vertexa Systems

Applying for Leave
1. Submit your leave application by email, well in advance for planned leave, and as soon as possible for unplanned/emergency leave.
2. Always mark your reporting manager AND the Program Management contact on all leave application emails. This is important because Program Management is responsible for sharing attendance records with clients, so keeping them informed ensures attendance data stays accurate.
3. This process applies to both planned and unplanned leave, with immediate effect.

Leave Calendar
- A shared leave calendar is maintained and circulated on a quarterly basis.
- All employees have view access to the leave calendar so they can check team availability before planning their own leave.
- Only the Project Management and HR teams have editing access, since they are responsible for updating and maintaining leave records.
- Employees should check the shared calendar and plan their leave accordingly, avoiding overlap with critical project milestones.

Types of Leave (standard practice - confirm specifics with HR)
- Casual Leave (CL): short-notice personal leave
- Sick Leave (SL): for illness, may require a medical certificate beyond a certain number of days
- Earned/Privilege Leave (EL/PL): planned leave accrued over the year
- Public Holidays: as per the official holiday calendar shared by HR at the start of the year

Attendance
- Attendance is tracked daily and shared with clients where applicable, so timely and accurate leave marking directly affects client-facing reporting.
- Employees should regularly check the leave calendar and confirm their attendance status if uncertain.

Who to Contact
- For leave balance queries or policy clarifications: HR team
- For attendance/calendar access issues: Project Management team
