IT Setup Guide - Vertexa Systems

1. Laptop and Account Setup
New employees typically receive a laptop from the IT desk on or before Day 1. Your official company email ID is usually created a day before your joining date. Check with HR/IT if you have not received your login credentials by your first day.

2. VPN Access
To connect to the company VPN:
- Download the approved VPN client from the IT Portal (e.g., GlobalProtect or equivalent, as instructed by IT).
- Log in using your employee ID/official email as the username.
- If login fails, first confirm you are connected to a stable internet connection, then contact the IT Helpdesk.

3. Software and Tool Access Requests
- Standard software (e.g., VS Code, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, browsers) is typically pre-installed on your laptop.
- For additional software or access to internal tools (e.g., Jira, GitHub, cloud consoles), raise a request through the IT Portal under "Software/Access Request."
- Access to project-specific tools (AWS console, database consoles, monitoring dashboards) is usually granted by your reporting manager or the relevant Team Lead once you are assigned to a project.

4. Password Policy
- Use a strong password combining uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords should be changed periodically as per company policy (check with IT for the exact reset interval).
- Never share your password or login credentials with anyone, including colleagues.

5. Common IT Issues and Fixes
- Forgot password: use the "Forgot Password" option on the login portal; resets are usually processed within a short time.
- VPN not connecting: check your network connection first, then restart the VPN client before raising a ticket.
- Software request pending: follow up with the IT Helpdesk if there is no update within the expected turnaround time.

6. Raising an IT Ticket
For any hardware, software, or access issue not covered above, raise a ticket via the IT Portal or email the IT Helpdesk directly, and CC your reporting manager for visibility.
