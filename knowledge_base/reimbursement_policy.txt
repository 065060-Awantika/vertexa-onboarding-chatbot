Reimbursement Policy - Vertexa Systems

Submission Deadline
- All reimbursement claims must be submitted by the 20th of every month.
- Employees should share their reimbursement details/receipts accordingly before this cutoff to ensure timely processing.

What Can Typically Be Claimed (standard practice - confirm exact categories with Finance/HR)
- Local travel for office/client work (cabs, fuel reimbursement where applicable)
- Business-related food/meal expenses during official travel or extended work hours
- Internet/mobile reimbursement if applicable to your role
- Any pre-approved client-visit related expenses

How to Submit a Claim
1. Collect original receipts/invoices for all expenses.
2. Fill out the reimbursement claim (format/template shared by HR or Finance).
3. Submit before the 20th of the month to ensure it is processed in that month's cycle.
4. Claims submitted after the cutoff may be processed in the following month's cycle.

Approval Process
Reimbursement claims are typically reviewed by your reporting manager first, then forwarded to Finance/HR for processing and payout, usually along with the monthly payroll cycle.

Who to Contact
For questions on reimbursement categories, limits, or claim status, contact the Finance/HR team.
